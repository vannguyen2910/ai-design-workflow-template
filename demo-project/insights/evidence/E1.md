**This is not evidence from real users.** The researcher, through an AI tool, played three personas on the prototype. The process and the tools were the same as a real round; the result is only a hypothesis to be confirmed. Details: `work/original-docs/13_Simulated_Usability_Findings.md`.

## What was done
Three personas (a planner, a loose traveller and a first-timer) each went through five tasks: discover, plan, connect, remember, share. After each task, a 7-point ease question; after the last, a 10-question usability scale. The researcher recorded where each persona paused, backtracked or gave up.

## Results at a glance
- Mean ease: 4.6 out of 7. Target is 5.5.
- Mean usability: 69.2 out of 100. Target is 68.
- Two tasks could not be finished: finding a specific local, and sharing with someone outside the list.

## What this does not show
What real travellers do. All three personas share one author, so they agree more than real people would.
