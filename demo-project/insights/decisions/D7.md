## Why
Reduces the effort of recording while users keep control (SP-J1 to SP-J4). **Tension recorded:** automatic recording needs always-on location permission, while privacy is a rising barrier to trying it (SP-A3).

## Options considered
- A manual diary with prompts.
- Automatic recording, public by default.
- Automatic recording, private by default (chosen).

## Trade-off accepted
Always-on location is needed for it to work, and it conflicts with the privacy barrier to trying the app.
