## Why
Value must be seen before asking for sign-up (SP-A4; PRD EA4-02, EA4-03). **Risk recorded:** the sign-up gate has not been validated because the prototype authenticates instantly.

## Options considered
- Ask for sign-up on first launch.
- Allow browsing, ask on Save or Connect (chosen).
- Allow everything and never require an account.

## Trade-off accepted
Fewer sign-ups at the start, in exchange for value being seen first. The gate cannot be tested until the prototype stops signing people in instantly.
