## Why
Near Me gives value right away, in context. If location permission has not been granted, use a personalised feed based on the travel style chosen in onboarding. Source: PRD for the Explore tab, requirements EX-01 to EX-03.

## Options considered
- Open on a Country list, like the big review sites.
- Open on the last viewed place.
- Near Me (chosen).

## Trade-off accepted
If location is off the screen falls back to a feed by travel style, which is less specific.
