## Why
Reverses the logic of TripAdvisor: it helps avoid places that are overcrowded or commercialised (SP-D3). **Not decided yet:** where the data for this signal comes from (user-reported or algorithmic).

## Options considered
- Star rating from contributors.
- A "popular" badge.
- Crowd signal in green, amber and red (chosen).

## Trade-off accepted
The signal needs data we do not have yet. If it is user-reported it can be gamed; if it is algorithmic it needs foot-traffic data.
