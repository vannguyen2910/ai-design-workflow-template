## Decision (proposed)
Should messaging a local only open after both sides show interest, and only between verified profiles in a free exchange? The team's current choice is a two-way exchange frame with verified profiles (D6).

## Insight
Travellers are open to asking a local for advice, but only when three things are true: they can see who the person is, no money is involved, and the two of them have something in common. Without these they stay with guidebooks and friends. This matters for the decision above, because each condition is a reason to say no if it is missing.

## Sources

| Source ID | Type | Verbatim quote | What it supports |
|---|---|---|---|
| SV-02 | Survey, free text | "I'd chat with a local if I knew they were real. A photo and a verified ID would do it." | Verified identity |
| SV-07 | Survey, free text | "The moment someone asks for money or a tip, it stops being a conversation." | No money |
| R02 | Store review (competitor app) | "Guide marketplace. Everyone is selling a tour. I just wanted a coffee recommendation." | No transaction wanted |
| F05 | Forum | "Met a local through a hobby group abroad. Best trip I ever had. We both liked climbing." | Shared interests |
| I01-06 | Interview P1 | "I'd message someone if they chose to be there and other travellers had left a note about them." | Voluntary locals, feedback from others |

Strength: medium. 5 notes from 4 kinds of source and the survey counts are high (12 of 13 open to it). All the notes come from the travellers' side; none comes from a local.

## What these sources do not tell us
- Whether locals want to take part. No local was asked, and the whole feature depends on them.
- What people do when they actually get a message from a stranger: every quote is what people say they would do.
- Whether "open to it" means they would do it. 12 of 13 is a stated attitude from a small, young sample.

## Challenge
1. **Where we say more than the data:** "free". Only SV-07 and R02 say no money. In the survey 9 of 13 say it, which leaves 4 who did not.
2. **Source that can mislead:** F05. A good story about one meeting through a hobby group, not through an app, so it supports shared interests but not an in-app exchange.
3. **Other explanations:**
   - People want a safe first message, not a local: SV-02 asks mostly for proof the person is real.
   - People want advice from other travellers, not locals: I01-06 trusts "other travellers" notes.
   - People like the idea but would not use it on a trip: 12 of 13 are open but only 3.38/5 call it important.
4. **Data that disagrees or was left out:** the importance score is lower than for planning and local tips, and it is not in the table above.
5. **New data that would make the insight wrong:** locals who say they would not take part, or travellers who get a reply and do not follow through.

**What changed our mind:** keep the insight at medium confidence. Local interviews come first, before more design work on the profile.

**Dissent kept:** the three other explanations. The first one in particular suggests a smaller feature (a safe, verified first message).

## Assumption map
- Trust is the main barrier to connecting: Confirmed on a small sample. 11 of 13 want a verified identity; I01-06 asks for feedback from other travellers.
- Travellers want non-transactional contact: Confirmed on a small sample. 9 of 13 and two notes.
- Locals are willing to take part: Unknown. Not tested.

## Why it ranks here
A strong need, but the importance score is lower (3.38/5) and it only works when the supply side (locals) takes part, which has not been researched.
