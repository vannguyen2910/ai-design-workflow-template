Source: Journal session notes, screen S30. Original level in the project: Critical (converted to 4 on the 0 to 4 scale).

## What changes

## How it showed up
The persona tried to share the journal with a friend who was not one of the three listed people. There was no field to type a name or send an invitation. She said: "So only these three can ever see it?" and abandoned the share step.

## Why this severity
Sharing was blocked outright for anyone outside the list, and sharing privately is the point of the private-by-default design.
