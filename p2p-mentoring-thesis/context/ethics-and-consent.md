## 1. What SRH requires

From the SRH "Preparing for your Master Thesis" briefing (in `work/thesis-brief/`):

- Ethics must be built into the **planning phase**, not added at the end.
- Seven principles: informed consent, prevention of harm, maximising benefits, justice (fairness), data protection and confidentiality, accountability and transparency, scientific integrity.

## 2. Principles applied to this project

| Principle | What it means here | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Informed consent** | Participants know the purpose, what is recorded, how data is used, and that they can withdraw | Consent form per method (section 5) |
| **Prevention of harm** | Interviewees may talk about money, job insecurity or their employers | Allow skipping any question. Anonymise by default. No identifying employer details in the paper without permission |
| **Maximising benefits** | Participants should get something back | Offer a short summary of the findings. Tell them how the work helps mentors and learners |
| **Justice** | Include different domains, experience levels, and both Vietnam and international participants. Do not exclude people who struggle with English | Offer the Vietnamese version of the consent and survey. Document your sampling choices |
| **Data protection** | Interviews contain personal data and voice recordings | See section 3 |
| **Accountability and transparency** | Be able to show what you did and why | Log every step in `work/research-log.md`. Keep signed or recorded consent |
| **Scientific integrity** | No invented data, no cherry-picking quotes | Keep raw data. Report negative findings. Cite sources in the source library |

## 3. Data protection (GDPR and Vietnam)

**Why GDPR applies:** You are studying in Germany at a German university and are the data controller for the data you collect. GDPR applies to the processing, including for participants outside the EU. `[verify with supervisor]`

**Vietnam layer:** Participants in Vietnam are also covered by Vietnamese data protection rules. As of my knowledge this includes Decree 13/2023/ND-CP and the newer Law on Personal Data Protection. `[verify the current text and effective date]` In practice, the consent steps in section 5 meet both: clear purpose, explicit consent, right to withdraw, minimal data.

### Data minimisation and storage plan

| Item | Plan |
|---|---|
| **Data collected** | Interviews: audio or video, transcript, role and domain, country. Survey: answers, no names, no email unless needed for the summary. Testing: screen and audio recording, task notes |
| **Identifiers** | Replace names with codes (`P01`, `P02`…). Keep the code-to-name list in a separate file |
| **Where it is kept** | One encrypted or access-restricted folder. Avoid personal devices without a password or disk encryption `[decide]` |
| **Transcription tools** | If you use an AI or cloud transcription tool, tell participants and check where the data is processed. Prefer a tool you can set not to train on the data `[decide]` |
| **Retention** | Delete recordings after transcription and checking, or at the latest after the thesis is graded. Keep anonymised transcripts for the thesis annex `[confirm retention period with the supervisor]` |
| **Sharing** | Annex only anonymised excerpts. Never share raw recordings |
| **Participant rights** | Access, correction, withdrawal, deletion. Provide your email as the contact |
| **Minors** | Do not include participants under 18 |

## 4. Recruitment rules

- Voluntary only, no pressure. Do not recruit people who depend on you (students you mentor, people who report to you).
- State the time needed (interview about 45 min, survey about 8 min, test about 45 min).
- Compensation: none planned. Say so clearly. If you give a small thank-you, disclose it. `[decide]`
- Do not collect more data than the research questions need.

## 5. Consent form templates (adapt before use)

### 5.1 Interview consent (1.4)

```
Title: Research on peer-to-peer mentoring services
Researcher: <your name>, Master student, SRH Fernhochschule
Supervisor: <supervisor name>
Contact: <your email>

Purpose: This interview is part of my master thesis on how experts and
learners can be matched in mentoring services. 

What happens: A conversation of about 45 minutes, with questions about
your experience as a mentor or mentee. 

Recording: With your permission I record audio (and video if on a video
call) to make a transcript. The recording is deleted after
<retention period>. 

Your data: Your name will not appear in the thesis. I use a code (e.g. P03)
and describe you only by general role and domain. Quotes may be used
anonymously. The anonymised transcript may be included in the annex. 

Voluntary: You can skip any question, stop at any time, and withdraw your
data until <date> without giving a reason. 

Your rights: You can ask me to show, correct or delete your data (GDPR). 
Contact: <your email>.

[ ] I have read the information and agree to take part.
[ ] I agree to the audio/video recording.
[ ] I agree that anonymised quotes may be used in the thesis.
[ ] I would like a summary of the results (email: ________)

Name / code: ______________   Date: ____________   Signature or "I agree" (email/recorded)
```

### 5.2 Survey consent (1.5): first page of the survey

```
This anonymous survey is part of a master thesis at SRH Fernhochschule
on peer-to-peer mentoring services. It takes about <8> minutes. 
Participation is voluntary. You can stop at any time and answers are
anonymous: no name or email is collected unless you choose to leave
one for the results summary. Data is stored securely and used only for
this thesis. Contact: <your email>.

[ ] I am 18 or older, I have read this information and I agree to take part.
```

### 5.3 User-testing consent (4.2)

```
Same as 5.1, plus:
- You are testing a prototype, not being tested yourself. There are no
  wrong answers.
- Screen and voice are recorded to study how the prototype works.
- Please do not enter real personal or payment data into the prototype.
- You can stop at any time.
```
