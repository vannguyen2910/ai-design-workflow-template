## Summary
Proposes an AI-Mediated Communication (AI-MC) framework to ease the **first contact** between mentors and mentees in online learning, using large language models inside a chat prototype (SparkGrow). Designed with Design Thinking and evaluated with Self-Determination Theory in a mixed-methods study.

## Credibility note
Peer-reviewed journal, 2026. Based on the abstract: small survey sample (N = 33), a prototype and not a live service, and a text-only chat. Check in the full text: where participants came from, how they were recruited, and whether interviews were also run.

## How I use it in the thesis
Evidence that technology can reduce friction in the matching and onboarding phase, which supports the thesis argument that mentoring is a matching and enablement problem. Also a design reference for the Develop phase (conversation support in the first contact).
