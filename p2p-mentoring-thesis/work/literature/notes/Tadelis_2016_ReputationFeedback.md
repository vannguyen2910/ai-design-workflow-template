## Summary
A survey article on how online marketplaces (eBay, Taobao, Uber, Airbnb) use reputation and feedback systems to create trust between strangers. It covers theory, how feedback systems work, empirical evidence, bias problems and design improvements.

## Credibility note
Peer-reviewed review in a leading annual-review series by a well-known platform economist. It is from 2016, so recent platform changes (AI-generated reviews, newer rating designs) are not covered. It is about product and ride platforms, not mentoring or education.
