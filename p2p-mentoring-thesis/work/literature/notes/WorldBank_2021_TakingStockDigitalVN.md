## Summary
Regular World Bank economic update for Vietnam with a special focus on the digital economy. It assesses connectivity, skills, innovation and information security using the CHIP framework (Connect, Harness, Innovate, Protect), and gives recommendations.

## Credibility note
Institutional, specialised on Vietnam, with the original data sources named in its figures. Weaknesses: data is from about 2020 to 2021, so it is dated for a 2026 thesis, and it was written during COVID-19. I could not read the numbers inside several tables and figures (for example the CHIP rankings and the group gaps in Table 2.1), so check them in the PDF.

## Evidence cards (page numbers from the text version; verify)
- Vietnam has fixed broadband and mobile subscriptions among the world's highest for its income level · Figures 2.4 and 2.5, p. 47 · Use in: tech-access
- Fixed-broadband speed lags peer countries; affordability data is given in Figures 2.6 to 2.9 · pp. 47-48 · Use in: tech-access
- Use of digital tools differs by income group and by location (urban vs rural) · Table 2.1, p. 48 · **numbers not read, check the table** · Use in: tech-access, Vietnam layer
- Qualified workers are scarce; the report says catching up with Thailand would take about 25 years at the current pace · Overview, p. xvi · Use in: tech-access (skills), economic factor
- Businesses find it hard to hire and keep data analysts and programmers · Overview, p. xvi · Use in: economic and social factors
- E-commerce is growing faster in Vietnam than globally; up to 60% of local businesses increased their online presence; over 2,000 public services were digitised · Fig. 2.1 p. 44, Overview p. xiii · Use in: tech-pace
- Digital platform use is mostly limited to simple business functions · Figure 2.20, p. 53 · Use in: tech-pace
- Cybersecurity performance is weak compared with peers; data portability is limited · Figure 2.22, p. 56; Overview, p. xvii · Use in: tech-data
- A draft Decree on Personal Data Protection was being finalised, and the report calls for a balance between data use and privacy · Overview, p. xvii · Use in: tech-data, Legal factor (link)
- Recommendations: build digital skills (including combined skill, finance and **mentorship** programmes for entrepreneurs, and soft skills), nurture innovative capacity (shift support from R&D toward technology adoption), improve information access, quality and security · Overview, pp. xvi-xvii · Use in: tech-pace, thesis relevance
