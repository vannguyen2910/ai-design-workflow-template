## Summary
Tests whether Google Gemini can support the myIDP career planning tool for STEM graduate students (mostly from underrepresented groups). Students tried 39 prompts and gave feedback. The authors conclude that AI can supplement but not replace human mentors, and propose two hybrid mentoring models.

## Credibility note
Peer-reviewed, with IRB approval. Small sample (18 students, one US university plus a few international visitors), mentee view only, free Gemini version only, data collected Jan to May 2024, and the two models are conceptual and untested. Participants received a $100 gift card.
