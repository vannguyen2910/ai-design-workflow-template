**Goal:** Know the market, the players and the real pain points before defining anything.
**Paper link:** Chapter on context and background, literature review, and the research methodology.

### 1.1 Desk research
- [ ] Define search terms and the source types (peer-reviewed, industry reports, official statistics)
- [ ] **PESTEL** across 6 factors, global level, then a Vietnam layer for each factor
  - [ ] Technological (5 subtopics): `#tech-ai` AI and automation · `#tech-access` digital access · `#tech-platform` platform technology · `#tech-pace` pace of innovation · `#tech-data` data, privacy and security
  - [ ] Political · [ ] Economic · [ ] Social · [ ] Environmental · [ ] Legal
- [ ] Rate each finding by impact and likelihood, and keep the top 2 to 3 per factor
- [ ] **Stakeholder map** (mentors, mentees, platform, payment providers, regulators, employers, schools)
- [ ] **System map** (value, money, information and trust flows between stakeholders)
- [ ] Write the "so what" for each output
- **Output:** PESTEL table, stakeholder map, system map, annotated source list
- **Notes:**

### 1.2 Lean UX Canvas
- [ ] Fill in the business problem, the business outcomes, the users and their benefits
- [ ] List the assumptions and turn the riskiest ones into testable hypotheses
- **Output:** One filled canvas, a list of hypotheses to validate in 1.4, 1.5 and Delivery
- **Notes:**

### 1.3 Competitor analysis
- [ ] Pick 6 to 8 competitors: ADPList, Preply, iTalki, Codementor, MentorCruise, Superpeer, Clarity.fm, plus Vietnam-local players
- [ ] Compare on domain scope, matching approach, pricing, trust mechanisms, mentor onboarding and long-term engagement
- [ ] Identify the gap (domain-agnostic matching, mentor enablement)
- **Output:** Comparison matrix, positioning map, gap statement
- **Notes:**

### 1.4 Expert interviews (qualitative)
- [ ] Write the interview guide, with questions linked to the hypotheses from 1.2
- [ ] Recruit 6 to 10 participants (SRH briefing: 5+ interviews) (mentors from different domains, some mentees, ideally some from Vietnam). **Start recruiting by 26 Oct.**
- [ ] Get consent, run, record and transcribe the interviews
- [ ] Code the transcripts and cluster the themes (affinity map)
- **Output:** Guide, transcripts, coded themes, key quotes
- **Notes:**

### 1.5 Survey (quantitative)
- [ ] Define the research questions and the target group (mentors and mentees, global plus Vietnam)
- [ ] Draft the questionnaire and pilot it with 3 to 5 people
- [ ] Choose the sample-size target (SRH briefing: 100+ respondents; suggest aiming for 100 to 150) and the distribution channels
- [ ] Collect, clean and analyse (descriptive statistics, group comparison, Vietnam versus global)
- **Output:** Questionnaire, dataset, charts, summary of findings
- **Notes:**

**Phase check (before moving on):**
- [ ] Insights from 1.1 to 1.5 are written up in 1 page
- [ ] Draft of the Context and Methodology sections exists
