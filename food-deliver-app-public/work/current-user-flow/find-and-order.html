Find and order food

Grab food delivery, Vietnam · iPhone · app v33437965 · English interface · walk-through of 23 Nov 2026

Possible breakdowns

These are hypotheses from my own walk-through of the app. They are not test findings. Each one is to be checked in the heuristic evaluation and the usability test.

  1. 1The Grab home screen shows many services and promotions at once, so users may not see the food entry at first.
  2. 2The delivery address is shown only as a small label such as Home near the top, so users may not notice it before ordering.
  3. 3Search results mix sponsored listings marked Ad with regular ones, and users may not tell them apart.
  4. 4Option names written by the restaurant appear in Vietnamese inside an English interface, and required and optional choices look alike.
  5. 5The menu opens with last order, offers and recommendations before the full menu, which may make a specific item harder to find.
