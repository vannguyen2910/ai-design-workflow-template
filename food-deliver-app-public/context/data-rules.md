# Data rules

*Three lines, in your own words. Drafted from our discussion: **to confirm** by the owner.*

1. What I am allowed to paste into an AI tool: my own notes and plans, text I wrote, the assignment brief, anonymised test data, and screenshots of the app with personal data blurred.
2. What I will not paste (or only paste after anonymising): participant names, recordings, raw session notes, and any account, address, phone number or payment details. These stay in `work/sources/_raw-private/`.
3. Which tool I use for what: Claude helps with structure, planning, explaining concepts and language editing. It does not collect or analyse my data, write my findings, or choose my sources. Every AI use is logged in `work/ai-use-log.md`, and the source rules are in `context/source-rules.md`.
