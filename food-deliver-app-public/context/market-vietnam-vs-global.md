## What this means for the paper

- Cash is still common in Vietnam, which supports your point about flexible payment, but the food-specific figure may be old and needs a newer source.
- Grab holds about half of the Vietnam market in one report, which is useful when you explain that Grab is a large provider.
- The global figures disagree too much to use without choosing one report and explaining its definition. Market reports and news articles are weak sources for an academic paper. Look for peer-reviewed studies or official statistics to back them up.
- I found nothing on how users in Vietnam receive orders at houses versus apartment buildings. That needs a source or your own test data.
