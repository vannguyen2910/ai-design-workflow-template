# Product and users

*Half a page, anonymised. This file goes into the AI steps first, so write it for a reader who knows nothing about the project.*

## The product

Grab's food delivery in Vietnam lets people find food and drinks from restaurants and have them delivered to their door, so they do not need to go out to buy them. It is one part of Grab's larger ecosystem of services, and this project looks only at the food delivery journey.

## The users

Anyone aged about 18 to 50: students, people at work, and retired people. Ordering food is a common, everyday need, so the owner does not limit the group by age. This is the owner's working assumption, not a research finding.

Their main goal is to reach restaurants that are not near them and to get food without leaving home. It suits people who are busy with work and have no time to cook. Typical situations are dinner at home and food late at night when no one wants to cook.

## Where it stands now

The owner has used the app in Vietnam on an iPhone, with the English interface (app version v33437965), and has taken screenshots of the whole journey from end to end: discovering the app, choosing a restaurant, picking the food, paying, and tracking the delivery. These screens have not been analysed yet.

## What success might look like

*assumed: the owner's first ideas, to be tested by the research.*

- Users can explore restaurants, not only the ones they already know.
- New restaurants can reach users more easily, and users find it easy to try a restaurant once or become loyal to it.
- Payment is smooth: little manual input, one-time payment, quick connection to a payment method.
- Tracking the delivery is easy.

## What I do not know

- What the best experience for this journey looks like. This is for the research to find out.
- Who the real users are in Vietnam, and how well the age and lifestyle assumptions above hold.
- How the app handles discovering new restaurants and rewarding loyal customers today.
- Which payment methods the app offers in Vietnam and how many steps they need.
- Which problems users actually meet in the three functions: finding and ordering, checking out and paying, and tracking the order.
