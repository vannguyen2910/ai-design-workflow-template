# Source rules

*This is an academic paper. Every claim needs a valid, current source or your own data. The AI follows these rules in every step.*

1. **Every claim has a source.** Definitions, method descriptions, theory and statements about users need a citation. Results and findings come only from your own data: your walk-through of the app, your evaluation and your usability tests.
2. **Citation style.** APA 7 with page numbers, at most two pages per reference, as the brief requires.
3. **Valid sources.** Use peer-reviewed articles, established textbooks, recognised standards and primary sources. Do not cite AI tools, blog posts of unclear origin or anonymous pages. The university's study materials are supporting material only and are not cited directly.
4. **Current sources.** Prefer recent work. A foundational work, such as the original source of a method, may be older, but mark it as foundational. **To confirm:** the oldest publication year you accept for other sources.
5. **No inventing.** The AI never makes up a citation, a page number, a quote, a figure or a participant result. If it does not know, it says so.
6. **When unsure, ask.** If the AI cannot verify a source, a year, a page number or a claim, it asks you and marks the item **to confirm**. You confirm it against the original before it goes into the paper.
7. **The AI may suggest sources to look for, not sources to trust.** You open every suggested source, read the relevant pages and check the details yourself.
