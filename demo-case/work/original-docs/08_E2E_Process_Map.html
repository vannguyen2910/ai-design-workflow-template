How a trip moves through Travel Buddy

Each column is one actor: the traveler, Travel Buddy itself, a local, and a companion or contact. A line that crosses into another column is a handover. The pink lines are the ones between traveler and local.

1. How the parts of the app hand over to each other

Read the numbers on the arrows together with the list below the boxes.

2. Before the trip: getting started, Discover and Plan

A visitor can browse without an account. Locals and other travelers supply the tips that fill the feed.

3. On the trip: arrival and Connect with a local

The traveler and the local are drawn side by side. The chat opens only when both sides have shown interest.

4. After the trip: Journal and the next trip

The journal stays private unless the traveler chooses contacts to share it with.

The map shows the main path. Error states and edge cases are not drawn.
