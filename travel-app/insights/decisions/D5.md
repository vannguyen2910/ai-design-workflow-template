## Why
P1 uses an AI chat tool to draft itineraries but the output has nowhere to go (SP-P6). Turn that output into an entry point to Travel Buddy. **Problem seen in the simulated test:** the parser kept the day label as the place name.

## Options considered
- Do not support AI itineraries.
- Import by pasting text (chosen).
- Connect directly to an AI chat tool.

## Trade-off accepted
Pasted text is messy, so the parser has to guess days and places. The test showed it guesses wrong at times.
