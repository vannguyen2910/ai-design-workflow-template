## Statement
We believe that if a local's profile has a verified identity, shared interests and a two-way, no-money exchange frame (SP-C1, SP-C2, SP-C3), users will message on the very first contact without hesitating.

## How we will test it
Task: contact a local for a recommendation. Watch the first message and ask where they hesitated.

## What would show we are wrong
People still hesitate at the profile, or cannot find the person they have in mind.
