## Statement
We believe that if each tip shows the identity, the travel style of the contributor and a crowd signal instead of a popularity badge (SP-D1, SP-D3), users will apply their own trust logic and interact with the first tip in under 30 seconds.

## How we will test it
First-use task: find a tip you would go to. Time the first interaction and ask what made them trust it.

## What would show we are wrong
People ignore the contributor and crowd signal, or take longer than 30 seconds to start.
