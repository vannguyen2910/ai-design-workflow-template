## Statement
We believe that if the journal records location and time by itself and then lays itself out (SP-J1, SP-J2), users will find it after the trip without being told it exists.

## How we will test it
Task after the simulated trip: "Show me what you remember of the trip." No mention of the journal.

## What would show we are wrong
Fewer than half open the journal without a hint.
