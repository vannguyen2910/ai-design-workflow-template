# AI use log

The brief requires a separate sheet after the cover that states how and how much AI was used and how the results were checked. Keep this log up to date as you work, so the sheet is written from facts and not from memory.

## How to log

After each time you use AI for the paper, add a row. Write what you asked, what it gave you, what you did with it (kept, edited or rejected), and how you checked it. Use `Not yet checked` until you have verified it yourself.

## Draft of the AI-use sheet

*Fill in at the end, from the log. Check that every statement is true.*

**Tools used:** Claude (Anthropic), ____________

**What AI was used for:** brainstorming, structuring the plan, explaining methods, formatting and language editing: ____________

**What AI was not used for:** collecting or analysing the test data, writing the findings, writing the recommendations, and choosing the sources. These are my own work.

**Extent:** about ____ % of the text was edited with AI help. The analysis, findings and recommendations were written by me.

**How I checked the AI output:** I read every AI-assisted text and rewrote it where needed. I checked each source, quote and page number against the original. I removed anything I could not verify. Results come only from my own walk-through, evaluation and tests.

## Rules I follow

- I decide. The AI proposes and I approve.
- The AI does not invent results, quotes, citations or page numbers.
- If the AI is unsure about a fact or source, it asks me and I confirm it against the original.
- AI tools are not cited as sources.
