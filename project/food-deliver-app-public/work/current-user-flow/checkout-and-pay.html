Grab food delivery, Vietnam · iPhone · app v33437965 · English interface · walk-through of 23 Nov 2026

Possible breakdowns

These are hypotheses from my own walk-through of the app. They are not test findings. Each one is to be checked in the heuristic evaluation and the usability test.

  1. 1The order summary puts the items, a promotion banner, fees and savings in one long screen.
  2. 2Priority and Standard delivery have the same price but different times, so the difference may be unclear.
  3. 3An expired card stays in the payment list, greyed out, next to methods that can be used.
  4. 4The add-card form warns that the card may be charged to check it, which may worry users.
  5. 5Many offers are greyed out because of a minimum spend, so it may be hard to see which ones apply.
