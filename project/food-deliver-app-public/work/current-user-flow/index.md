# Current user flow

One flow for each core function, drawn from the walk-through of 23 Nov 2026 (Grab app v33437965, iPhone, Vietnam, English interface).

| Flow | Covers |
|---|---|
| Find and order food | From opening the app to the basket |
| Check out and pay | From the order summary to placing the order, including changing the payment method |
| Track the order and resolve problems | From the order confirmation to rating the delivery, getting help or reordering |

Use the button above each diagram to show or hide the screen references. They point to the screenshots in `work/assets/`.

The numbered markers are possible breakdowns. They are hypotheses from the walk-through and must be checked in the heuristic evaluation and the usability test.
