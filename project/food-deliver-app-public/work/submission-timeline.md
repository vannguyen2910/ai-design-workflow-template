When you finish a phase, set **Status** to `Done` and write the date in **Completed on**. Use `In progress` while you work on it and `Not started` before that. Tell me "phase 4 is done" and I will update this file for you.

## Notes

- The weeks around 24 December to 1 January are likely to be slower. Phase 5 has extra time for that reason.
- Phases 9 and 10 overlap the others on purpose, so you write while you research instead of leaving all the writing to the end.
- Review this timeline every two weeks. If a phase is late, update the dates in this file.
- Keep raw data from testing in `work/sources/_raw-private/` only. Do not give it to an AI tool before it is anonymised.
- Keep personal notes in `_personal/`. They stay on your computer.
