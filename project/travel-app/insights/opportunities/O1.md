## Decision (proposed)
Which planning feature to build first? The team has two candidates in the plan area: one-tap save from any tip into the trip shortlist (D4) and importing an AI-written itinerary (D5).

## Insight
Travellers who plan a trip use 8 to 10 tools and copy between them by hand. Most plan loosely (9 of 13), but 6 of 13 still make a detailed schedule, so one place for planning has to work in both modes: detailed before the trip, flexible once there. This matters for the decision above.

|---|---|---|---|

Strength: medium. 5 notes from 4 kinds of source. The survey figures (planning rated 4.38/5; 5 of 13 name fragmentation) agree with the notes, but the sample is small.

## What these sources do not tell us
A sample of 13, skewed to young people in Asia, and one interview. They do not show how people plan once they are on the trip, because both sources are what people say, not what they do.

## Challenge
- **What changed our mind:** with 8 responses planning ranked last (3.00/5). With 13 it ranked first (4.38/5). The rank moved with five more answers, so it is not stable yet.
- **Dissent kept:** 6 of 13 still make a detailed schedule, so a loose, flexible plan alone does not serve them. Fragmentation ranks only third in the survey (5 of 13), yet P1 names it the most important fix.

## Assumption map (proposed, from the persona document)
- "Trip planning is a secondary feature": Unconfirmed. The survey (rank 1) and P1 point the other way.
- "App fragmentation is a key pain point": Confirmed on a small sample. 5 of 13 in the survey and P1 names it first.

## Why it ranks first
This is the feature given the highest importance score after the sample was expanded (from last rank at n=8 to rank 1 at n=13), and where P1 describes the pain most clearly: every step is a manual hand-over. The plan must serve both modes: detailed before the trip and flexible once there.
